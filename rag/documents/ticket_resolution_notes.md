# Ticket Resolution Notes

## TCKT-4821 — BFP-102 High Vibration, resolved 2026-07-12

Root cause: coupling misalignment found during vibration spectrum analysis
after two consecutive high-vibration alarms. Realigned coupling and replaced
worn spacer. Vibration returned to baseline. Recommend adding this asset to
the quarterly precision-alignment check list.

## TCKT-4790 — C-201 recurring High Discharge Pressure, resolved 2026-06-30

Root cause: downstream Unit 2 blending header control valve was cycling due
to a tuning issue in an unrelated loop, causing discharge pressure at C-201
to ride the alarm setpoint repeatedly over several days. No fault found on
the compressor itself. Closed after downstream loop was retuned by process
control engineering.

## TCKT-4715 — M-305 Motor Trip, resolved 2026-06-02
